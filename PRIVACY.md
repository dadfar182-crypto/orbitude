# Orbitude privacy policy

_Last updated: November 2026_

Orbitude does not collect, store on any server, sell or share any personal information.

**Location.** If you tap "Use my location", Orbitude asks your device for your approximate position. It is used only
on your device to calculate what is in your sky. It is saved only in the app's storage on your device so the app
remembers your place next time, and it is never sent to us or to anyone else. You can instead search for a place
by name, and you can turn off location access at any time in your device settings.

**Satellite close-ups.** When you open the Close-up view, the app downloads satellite photo tiles from Esri
(esri.com). As with any website, Esri receives your device's IP address and the map area being requested so it can
send those images. Orbitude does not send your saved location or any other information with these requests.
Esri's privacy statement: https://www.esri.com/en-us/privacy/overview

**No accounts, ads, analytics or tracking.** Orbitude has no sign-in, shows no ads, and contains no analytics or
tracking code. Everything except the Close-up view works offline.

**Children.** Orbitude is suitable for all ages and collects no information from anyone, including children.
